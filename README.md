# Memecoin Radar

Windows app that watches new Solana memecoins live, filters out scams and rings when a coin is tied to a fresh tweet by Elon, Trump and other big accounts. It also follows traders' wallets and alerts you on every buy.

## Install

1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `MemecoinRadar-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.
2. Double-click it. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the app is not code-signed yet).
3. Open **Memecoin Radar** from your desktop.
4. Go to **Settings**, connect an X account (a spare one, not your main account) by pasting its `auth_token` and `ct0` cookies, then **Save & restart**.

Updates install themselves: when a new version is out, an **Update ready** card appears in the sidebar.

## Pages

- **Radar**: new pump.fun coins scored live (dev buy, holders, RugCheck, buys, tweets). Snipes (coin launched on a VIP tweet) ring a loud alarm and copy the CA.
- **Wallets**: paste traders' wallets, see their buys and sells within seconds and check their real PnL.
- **Settings**: X account, VIP and watched accounts, filters, sounds.

> Not financial advice. Memecoins are extremely risky: most new coins go to zero.
